# Absolute Comics — Fan Expo Chicago 2026 Signing Plan

Collecting and signature-acquisition plan for DC's **Absolute Universe** line, built
around **FAN EXPO Chicago 2026**.

- **Show:** FAN EXPO Chicago 2026
- **Venue:** Donald E. Stephens Convention Center, Rosemont, IL
- **Dates:** August 14–16, 2026
- **Research captured:** July 29, 2026 (**16 days out**)

---

## The one thing to read first

The trip plan assumed *"three books to Snyder, three more to Artist Alley."* The first
half works. **The second half does not, as originally scoped** — and two of the four
properties on the target list should be dropped from the signing budget entirely.

**No artist from any Absolute title is booked for this show.**

| Property | Creative team | Signer at the show? |
|---|---|---|
| Absolute Batman | Scott Snyder (w) / Nick Dragotta (a) | ✅ **Snyder only** — Dragotta not attending |
| Absolute Catwoman | Che Grayson + **Scott Snyder** (w) / Bengal (a) | ✅ **Snyder only** — Bengal not attending |
| Absolute Wonder Woman | Kelly Thompson (w) / Hayden Sherman (a) | ❌ **Neither attending** |
| Absolute Green Arrow | Pornsak Pichetshote (w) / Rafael Albuquerque (a) | ❌ **Neither attending** |

Two consequences:

1. **Do not buy Absolute Wonder Woman or Absolute Green Arrow for signatures.** There is
   nobody at this show who can sign them as a credited creator. Buy them to read or to
   speculate on, but that is a separate decision from this trip.
2. **Snyder is the whole signing thesis.** He is the only credited Absolute creator in the
   building — and, usefully, he is credited on *both* remaining properties, because he
   co-writes Absolute Catwoman alongside Che Grayson.

See [`SIGNING-PLAN.md`](SIGNING-PLAN.md) for the six-book allocation and the math, and
[`PURCHASE-PLAN.md`](PURCHASE-PLAN.md) for what to order and when.

## The best single book on the list

**Absolute Batman #13** — the instinct to name this issue specifically was correct.

- **First appearance of Absolute Catwoman**
- Written by **Scott Snyder**, who is attending
- Released October 8, 2025 — cheap on the secondary market *right now* (roughly $10–20 for
  a Cover A in NM), because the market has not fully repriced it against the ongoing
  Absolute Catwoman series that launched June 2026
- Snyder is a credited creator on both the key issue **and** the spinoff it launched

A first-appearance key, signed by the writer who created the character, bought before the
spinoff series matures, is the highest value-per-dollar move available on this trip.

## Two corrections to the working assumptions

**The store is `comicselitecomics.com`, not `comicselite.com`.** The `.com` short domain is
a parked listing on a domain-sale site and sells nothing. The real Comics Elite storefront —
the Indianapolis retailer known for limited trade dress and virgin variants — is
**comicselitecomics.com**. All inventory in this repo comes from the real store.

**Absolute Batman #13 is a back issue, not a new release.** It shipped October 8, 2025. The
series is currently at **#22** (July 7, 2026), with **#23 on August 12, 2026** — two days
before the show.

**Comics Elite can't sell you the most important book on the list.** They're a new-release
exclusive-variant shop working on pre-order, not a back-issue dealer — their Absolute Batman
stock starts at **#19**. They *did* have a #13 exclusive (Ivan Talavera, shared with The 616
Comics), but the product page now 404s and the shared 616 Comics version is **sold out** at
$50.00. Absolute Batman **#13 and #1 both have to come from the secondary market**; everything
else on the buy list comes from Comics Elite as preferred.

## The Experience, confirmed

The **Greg Capullo & Scott Snyder Comic Experience** — the "Snyder Fan Experience" — includes
**3 witnessed signatures from Capullo and 3 from Snyder**, plus premium panel seating and
exclusive merch.

Two consequences that reshape the budget:

- **No per-autograph tickets — but the Experience ticket is a real, still-unpriced cost**
  (~$150–250 modeled ≈ $25–42 per witnessed signature). Book selection is now
  the only lever on value.
- **"Witnessed" means CGC Signature Series is live.** A witness present at signing is what
  makes the yellow SS label possible, and it can't be added later.

But the split is **3 and 3, not 6 Snyder**, and Capullo is credited on **no** Absolute title.
A dual signature consumes one slot from each. That constraint drives the allocation in
[`SIGNING-PLAN.md`](SIGNING-PLAN.md).

**The new dominant cost is grading, not comics.** CGC Signature Series Standard rose to
**$70/book** on January 6, 2026 — slabbing all six would cost **$420**, more than every comic
on this list combined. The recommendation is to slab **2–3 books ($140–210)** and keep the rest
raw. And because a 9.8 SS is worth a multiple of a 9.4 SS, **buy raw copies at NM 9.6+ only.**
